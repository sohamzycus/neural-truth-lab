# Review 3 — Design Critic Perspective

**Questions:** Does this feel exceptional? Generic? Memorable? Better than benchmark?

## Score: 8 / 10

## Strengths

- **Visual identity:** Deep-space indigo/cyan/violet palette distinct from generic AI-purple gradients
- **Signature system:** Token nodes, trade-off color coding (+/−/→), panel glow on key moments
- **Narrative spine:** 13 chapters with timeline as navigation — not card-grid taxonomy
- **Interaction philosophy:** Sliders and toggles teach (KV heads, causal mask, RoPE angles) — not decoration
- **Information density:** Expert mode adds tensor shapes without splitting into two apps
- **Family tree disclaimer:** "Map after the story" prevents taxonomy-first reading

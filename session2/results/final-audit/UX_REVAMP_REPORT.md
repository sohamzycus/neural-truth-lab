## B. SamaBPE USP

Multilingual **training-exposure weight search** over standard Hugging Face BPE: choose EN/HI/TE/BN weights → train real 10K tokenizer → validate encode/decode → measure four frozen Wikipedia corpora → score balance → iterate. Implemented in `scripts/run_faithful_weight_search.py`.
